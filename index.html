K-SPOTLIGHT DAILY

Five Korean entertainment updates, every day

K-Spotlight Daily is a YouTube Shorts channel that summarizes the day's most-reported Korean entertainment news (K-pop, K-drama, Korean film and celebrities) in about 30 seconds, in English, for international fans.

About the K-Spotlight Uploader app

K-Spotlight Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It prepares a short text-card video with AI narration from public news headlines and uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
