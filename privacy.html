Privacy Policy

K-Spotlight Uploader · Last updated: October 11, 2026

K-Spotlight Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the K-Spotlight Daily YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the owner's own channel.

Information the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services with a single permission (youtube.upload) to upload videos to the channel owner's YouTube account. It does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users or viewers.

How information is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer so the daily upload can run. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
